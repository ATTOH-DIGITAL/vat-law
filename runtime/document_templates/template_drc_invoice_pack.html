Regulatory note. The Construction Services Domestic Reverse Charge applies to most supplies of building and construction services reported through CIS, supplied to a UK VAT-registered customer who is not an end user or intermediary. This pack records vat.law's per-invoice determination. Regulated questions on end-user / intermediary status, scope of CIS, or contentious customer disputes are routed to the named CTA partner adviser.

4. End-user / intermediary determination

An end user is a UK VAT-registered customer that does not make onward supplies of construction services as part of the same site / project. An intermediary is connected to an end user (group, common ownership, common landlord). DRC does not apply where the customer has provided written end-user or intermediary notification before invoicing.
