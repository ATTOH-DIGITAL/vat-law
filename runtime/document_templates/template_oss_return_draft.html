{{customer_legal_name}} · One Stop Shop (Union Scheme) · Quarter {{period_label}}
Statutory basis. The One Stop Shop (OSS) Union scheme allows a UK-established trader registered for OSS in an EU Member State of Identification to declare and pay VAT on intra-EU B2C distance sales of goods and B2C services through a single quarterly return. The return must be filed and paid by the end of the month following the quarter end. This draft is prepared by vat.law from the customer's sales ledger; the named CTA partner adviser is the regulated adviser of record and the customer's sign-off is mandatory before submission to the Member State of Identification's portal.
