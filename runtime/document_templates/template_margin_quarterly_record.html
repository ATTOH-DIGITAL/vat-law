Statutory basis. Records prepared for the second-hand margin scheme (Notice 718), global accounting (Notice 718/2), or the Tour Operators' Margin Scheme (Notice 709/5). A trader using a margin scheme must keep a stock book in chronological order, calculate margin on each sale (or on the pool for global accounting), and account for VAT at the appropriate fraction of the margin. This record evidences compliance; regulated questions on scheme eligibility or scheme switches are routed to the named CTA partner adviser.
