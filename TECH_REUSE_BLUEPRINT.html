vat.law · Tech Reuse Blueprint

78% Supreme-stack reuse · £5,600 / 4-week launch · the cheapest complex-VAT navigator on the market

Headline

vat.law is the 25th vertical deployment off the Supreme Operating System. ~78% of the runtime is already written in sibling verticals (sole.tax, accounts.law, form.law, pension.law, apex_saas, immigration.law). The 22% new build is concentrated in five complex-VAT calculation engines (partial exemption · DRC · margin / TOMS · cross-border · HMRC enquiry) and the CTA partner-network plumbing. End-to-end cost £5,600 · end-to-end time 4 weeks · one founder + one India dev rotation.

Why this is cheap

The MTD plumbing — OAuth, obligation poll, fraud-prevention headers, digital-link records, 9-box submission — was built once for sole.tax (Vertical #7) and reused in landlord.tax (Vertical #8). vat.law is the third reuse of that same module. The partner-adviser routing logic was built once for immigration.law (Vertical #24, yesterday) and reused here. The doc factory was built once for apex.studio (Vertical #1) and reused 23 times. The marginal cost of adding the 25th vertical is the 22% net-new code — five calculation engines and four bookkeeping-software integrations — at £5,600 over four weeks.
