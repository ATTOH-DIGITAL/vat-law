How to read this. The reverse charge applies when a VAT- and CIS-registered sub-contractor supplies specified construction services to another VAT-registered business that has not notified you it is an end user or intermediary — then the customer accounts for the VAT, not you. Any one of those conditions failing sends it back to standard 20% VAT. Professional services (architecture, surveying) and materials-only supplies are outside CIS.  Scope.
