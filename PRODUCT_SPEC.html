vat.law · Product Spec

User journeys · feature spec · DB schema · API surface · 18 May 2026

1. Primary user journeys

Journey A · Construction sub-contractor on DRC. Customer signs up to Pro · connects QuickBooks · imports 90-day invoice history · vat.law classifies each invoice as standard / zero / DRC · returns invoice-by-invoice posting suggestions · auto-files Q1 return with reverse-charge fields populated · alerts customer 30/7 days before due date.
Journey B · Mixed retailer with partial exemption. Customer signs up to Pro · uploads chart of accounts + 12-month invoice history · vat.law detects exempt outputs (financial services / insurance commission) · runs standard partial-exemption method · returns residual input recovery percentage · generates methodology memo · India QA stamps · CTA partner signs adoption memo · customer presents to HMRC if challenged.
Journey C · E-commerce business on OSS. Customer signs up to Cross-Border · connects Xero + Shopify · vat.law detects EU B2C sales · generates OSS quarterly return draft · reconciles UK 9-box · flags <£135 IOSS consignments · pulls C79 from postponed VAT accounting · prepares EORI sanity check.
Journey D · HMRC enquiry response. Customer receives a pre-arranged-visit letter from HMRC · uploads PDF to vat.law · studio extracts officer · venue · subject area · response deadline · pulls 12-month audit trail from vat.law + connected bookkeeping software · drafts response pack · India QA stamps · CTA partner signs cover letter · Supreme Capital advance-financing referral fires on assessed liability over £5k.
Journey E · Penalty-point warning. Customer accumulates 3 of 4 quarterly points · vat.law dashboard turns amber · India QA emails the customer with the appeal grounds reference (Sch 24 FA 2007 reasonable excuse) · cross-sell Cross-Border tier offered if pattern suggests structural cause.
