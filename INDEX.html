The thesis in one paragraph. 2.179m UK businesses are VAT-registered. MTD VAT has been mandatory for all of them since April 2022. The basic-filing market is commoditised at £14–£33/mo (Xero / Sage / QuickBooks). What's not commoditised — partial exemption, reverse charge / DRC, margin schemes, OSS / IOSS, place-of-supply, postponed VAT accounting, capital goods scheme, TOMS, group VAT, HMRC enquiry response, the post-Jan-2023 points-based penalty regime — is where SMEs systematically overpay, under-claim or get penalised. That's where vat.law lives.

What makes this build cheap

vat.law is the 25th deployment off the Supreme Operating System. The MTD HMRC API plumbing already exists in sole.tax (Vertical #7) — OAuth, obligation poll, 9-box submission, digital-link audit. The cron pattern (90/60/30/7-day exposure alerts) already exists in pension.law. The doc factory already exists in apex.studio. The CTA partner-network architecture is a direct copy of the OISC partner pattern from immigration.law (Vertical #24, built yesterday). Net-new work is the complex-VAT calculation modules (partial exemption · DRC · margin · place-of-supply · TOMS · capital goods) and the HMRC enquiry response workflow.

Boundary reminder. vat.law is the technology platform and deterministic computation layer. Regulated complex VAT advice is the responsibility of the customer's CTA / CIOT / ATT partner adviser. The platform never gives advice that crosses into regulated tax-advisory territory — it routes to the partner network, with trained QA reviewer stamping every regulator-facing output. Commission on partner referrals is firewalled from the routing decision.
