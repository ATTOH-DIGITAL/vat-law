Regulatory boundary reminder. vat.law is a technology platform — MTD VAT submission, deterministic complex-VAT computation, audit trail. It is not a regulated tax adviser on customer specific facts. All regulated complex-VAT advice (partial-exemption methodology agreement, TOMS election, CGS dispute, HMRC enquiry response, voluntary disclosure) is routed to the customer's CTA / CIOT / ATT partner adviser. Every regulator-facing output requires India QA pre-screen and CTA partner sign-off — never auto-send. See CLAUDE.md and SNAP_MANIFEST.json → regulatory_boundaries.
